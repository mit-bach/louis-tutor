## Entry template

```
### YYYY-MM-DD · <file and section>
- **What happened:** the behaviour he didn't like, and in which course or lesson.
- **His guess at the cause:** the file or rule he named. Hit / miss, and the real cause.
- **Old rule:** verbatim.
- **New rule:** verbatim.
- **His words:** verbatim.
- **Scope:** this course / every course.
- **His prediction:** what he expects the next lesson to do differently.

```
